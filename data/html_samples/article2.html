Astronomers detect unusual signal from nearby star

By Science Desk — May 4, 2026

A team of astronomers reported today that they have detected a periodic radio signal from a star approximately 12 light-years from Earth. The signal, captured over six months of observation, shows an unusual regularity that has prompted further investigation.

The researchers cautioned that natural explanations remain the most likely, and that follow-up observations from other facilities are needed before any firm conclusion can be drawn. A preprint has been posted to arXiv.

The discovery has reignited discussion about the merits of long-running surveys versus targeted searches.
