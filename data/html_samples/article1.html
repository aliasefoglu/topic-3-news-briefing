Acme unveils new AI processor

By Sample Reporter — May 4, 2026

Acme Corp announced a new processor today aimed at AI workloads. The chip, code-named "Maple", features a redesigned memory hierarchy and is the first in the company's lineup to use 3 nm process technology.

Industry analysts welcomed the announcement, noting that energy efficiency has become the dominant axis of competition in the AI accelerator market. The chip is expected to ship in volume in the third quarter.

Acme did not disclose pricing.
